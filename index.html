01. About Me

Hello! I'm Alex, a software developer based in San Francisco, CA. I enjoy creating things that live on the internet — whether that be websites, applications, or anything in between.

My interest in web development started back in 2012 when I decided to try building a custom blog theme. Turns out hacking things together taught me a lot about HTML, CSS, and JavaScript!

Fast-forward to today, I've had the privilege of working at a start-up, a large corporation, and a design studio. My main focus is building accessible, inclusive products and digital experiences at TechCorp.
