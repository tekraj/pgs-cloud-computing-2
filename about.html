About Me

A little bit about who I am and what I do.

→ Who I Am

Hi! I'm Alex Morgan, a full-stack developer and problem solver based in San Francisco, CA. With over 5 years of professional experience, I specialize in turning complex requirements into clean, performant web applications.

I started my journey in web development back in 2012, tinkering with HTML and CSS to customize blog themes and personal pages. What began as curiosity quickly turned into passion when I discovered JavaScript — and I've never looked back.

Today I work across the full stack: designing intuitive user interfaces with React and Vue.js, building scalable APIs with Node.js and Django, and deploying cloud-native applications on AWS and GCP. I care deeply about code quality, automated testing, and developer experience.

Outside of code, I'm an avid rock climber, coffee snob, and open-source contributor. I occasionally write about web development on my blog and mentor junior developers through local tech community events.

→ What I Do

A broad view of my areas of expertise.

Frontend Development

Building responsive, accessible interfaces with React, Vue.js, and modern CSS. From design systems to micro-animations, I craft UIs that delight users.

Backend Development

Designing RESTful and GraphQL APIs with Node.js, Express, and Django. Focused on scalability, security, and clean architecture.

Cloud & DevOps

Deploying and managing infrastructure on AWS and GCP. Comfortable with Docker, CI/CD pipelines, and infrastructure-as-code.

Every project I build is mobile-first and tested across devices. I also develop cross-platform mobile apps using React Native.

Performance & Security

Auditing and optimising for Core Web Vitals, implementing OWASP security best practices, and ensuring reliable CI/CD test coverage.
